title: The sky outside, behind the glass
---

Behind the glass stands the sky as it is outside right now — the sun where it stands and as bright as the hour, the season and the place make it, the moon lighting the glass at night, the stars coming out — worked out from the device's time zone, with nothing asked for or sent. Tilt the phone and the sky stays level with the world; pick a fixed day, dusk or night in Settings instead.
